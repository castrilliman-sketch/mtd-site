## Recommendation: approach these first
1. **Taxfix**: closest match to "someone do MTD for me" (accountant reviews and submits each quarter), clear £25 fee. Ask them to confirm MTD subscriptions count.
2. **The Accountancy Partnership**: highest published sole-trader fee (£80+), custom landing page and a 10% reader discount make it a strong "recommended accountant" option.
3. **Unbiased**: covers readers who want a local accountant; £20 per accepted enquiry; apply through Impact alongside the Sage application.

Direct deals with firms: feasible once the site shows traffic. Offer a monthly "featured MTD accountant" slot or a fixed fee per qualified introduction. Firms quote their own fees; we have not verified any market rate, so don't promise numbers. ICAEW/ACCA members must follow their institute's rules on disclosing referral fees and commissions to clients, which the firm handles on their side.

## Regulatory and compliance notes
- **Accountancy referrals are not FCA-regulated**: tax and accountancy services aren't regulated activities, so linking to accountants or selling introductions needs no FCA permission.
- **Stay away from anything that is**: loans to pay tax bills (credit broking), tax-investigation, landlord or other insurance (insurance distribution), and mortgage, pension or investment advice. Unbiased also lists financial advisers and mortgage brokers. Keep our links to its accountant category, and only use its approved creatives. Promoting regulated financial services can be a financial promotion under s21 FSMA.
- **Advertising rules (ASA/CAP)**: affiliate and paid placements must be obviously labelled. The site already does this.
- **Data protection (UK GDPR / PECR)**: prefer link-based referrals, where the reader contacts the firm themselves. If we ever pass a reader's details to a firm, we need a separate, unticked, specific consent naming who receives them, plus a privacy notice update. The email list consent does not cover this.
