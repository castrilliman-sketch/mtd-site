HMRC recognises well over 100 products for MTD for Income Tax, and it doesn't recommend any of them. Here's a quick way to narrow it down.

**Check these four things first**
- **Your income types:** sole trader, UK property, foreign property. Not every product covers all three.
- **Tax return as well as updates:** some products only send quarterly updates, so you'd need a second product or an agent for the tax return.
- **How you keep records now:** spreadsheet users can look at bridging software; others may want bank feeds.
- **The real price after any introductory offer.** Many products advertise a discount for the first few months.

**Is free enough?** HMRC says free products are available for people with simple tax affairs, but they may have limits, such as a set number of transactions. Our directory shows which products HMRC lists with a free version.

**Landlords:** use the "Landlord (UK property)" filter, and the "Joint property (confirmed)" filter if you own with someone else.
**Sole traders:** use the "Sole trader" filter, and "CIS ready" if you work in construction.
