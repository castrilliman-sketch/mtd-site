Plenty of people will handle Making Tax Digital themselves. Others would rather pay someone. Here's a quick way to decide.

**Doing it yourself usually works well if:**
- you already keep your records up to date every month or so
- you have one business or a few straightforward properties
- you're comfortable with an app or spreadsheet

**An accountant or bookkeeper may be worth it if:**
- you have several income sources, foreign property or CIS deductions
- your records tend to pile up until January
- you'd rather spend the time on your business

An agent can sign you up for MTD, send your quarterly updates and submit your tax return for you. HMRC explains how agents work with MTD in its guidance for agents. If you use one, they can also tell you whether standard or calendar update periods suit you.

**Tip:** ask any accountant you're considering what software they use and whether their fee covers the four quarterly updates as well as the tax return.
