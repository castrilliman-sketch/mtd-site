Hi {{ contact.FIRSTNAME }},

Most of the work in Making Tax Digital happens once, at the start. Here are the five decisions to make before your first quarterly update.

**1. Which tax year you start.** Check your qualifying income (self-employment plus property income, before expenses) on page 1 of your kit. If HMRC expects you to qualify it writes to you, but HMRC says it's still your responsibility to check if no letter arrives.

**2. Standard or calendar update periods.** Standard periods follow the tax year (6 April to 5 April) and are the default. Choose calendar periods (1 April to 31 March) only if that's your accounting period. You must choose before your first update, because you can't switch for the rest of that tax year.

**3. Software that covers all your income types.** Check it handles self-employment, UK property and foreign property if you need them, and whether it can also submit your tax return. Some products only send quarterly updates.

**4. Spreadsheet or full software.** If you already keep good records in a spreadsheet, "bridging" software can send your updates from it. If not, full bookkeeping software with bank feeds may save time.

**5. Jointly owned property.** For jointly let property you can send income only during the year and add expenses at the end, by resending your fourth update before your tax return.
