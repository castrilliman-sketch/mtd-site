Why "free" needs a second look

HMRC doesn't make its own software. GOV.UK says you need commercial software that can keep digital records, send quarterly updates and submit your tax return by 31 January after the tax year. On price, it says free products are available "for those with simple tax affairs but there may be limits on how the product can be used, for example they could have a limited number of transactions".

Source: GOV.UK, Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

If you started MTD in April 2026, your first tax return sent through MTD software is the 2026 to 2027 return, due by 31 January 2028. Software that only sends quarterly updates means you'd need a second product for that return.

The common catches, with examples

  • Transaction or invoice limits. Sage Sole Trader Free says it includes 25 auto-categorised transactions a month, one connected bank account and 5 sales invoices. (Sage)
  • No bank feeds. Clear Books' Sole Trader Free plan is described as "for MTD only"; automated bank feeds are on its £5 a month plan. Its Foreign Property Free plan covers one property. (Clear Books)
  • A time limit. RentalBux says its free plan is free until March 2028, and that splitting jointly owned property is on paid plans. (RentalBux)
  • A unit limit. PaTMa's free plan covers one rental unit. (PaTMa)
  • Free only with a bank account. FreeAgent is free while you keep a qualifying NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland or Ulster Bank business current account, or a Mettle account with at least one transaction a month. (FreeAgent)
  • Free to use, paid to submit. Landlord Studio's MTD Go plan is free for up to 3 properties but charges £5 per MTD submission. HMRC doesn't mark it as a free version. (Landlord Studio)
  • Tax return "coming soon". Aligned.tax says filing your own quarterly updates is free for individuals, and lists annual filing as coming soon. HMRC's listing shows quarterly updates only. (Aligned.tax)

Questions worth answering before you choose

GOV.UK suggests checking that software:

  1. supports all your MTD income sources (self-employment, UK property, foreign property; see MTD and foreign property)
  2. lets you send quarterly updates and submit your tax return
  3. lets you report your other income (for example a salary, pension or savings) on the tax return
  4. works with your update period (standard tax year or calendar quarters; see the difference)

It also mentions HMRC Assist, support for more than one agent, and whether the software will still fit if your income sources change. For a free plan, it's also worth asking what happens if you go over a limit, and whether the free plan has an end date.
