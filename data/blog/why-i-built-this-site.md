I've spent 20 years working in finance in London. That time has been in debt purchasing, complaints handling and credit control for a consumer loan company, and I've also trained and managed teams of new starters.

What that gave me was a view from both sides. I've seen how businesses and regulators talk, and I've seen how hard that language can be for the people on the receiving end. Technical wording that makes sense inside an office can leave someone unsure what they actually have to do.

[Making Tax Digital for Income Tax](/do-i-need-mtd/) is a big change for landlords and sole traders. Whether you need to use it, and when, depends on your qualifying income: your self-employment and property income added together, before expenses. People with qualifying income over £50,000 in the 2024 to 2025 tax year should already be using it. From 6 April 2027 it applies if that income was over £30,000 in 2025 to 2026, and from 6 April 2028 if it was over £20,000 in 2026 to 2027. If your qualifying income is £20,000 or less, you are automatically exempt. Once you are in, you keep digital records and send quarterly updates, which are due on 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May.

Those dates and thresholds are on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax). What is harder to find is a clear way to choose software. HMRC publishes a list of recognised products, and it does not recommend any of them. The list tells you what is compatible, not what will suit your situation. A lot of what is written about that choice is either jargon, or a sales pitch for one product.

I built this site to close that gap. Here you'll get plain English, the facts checked against GOV.UK and the software providers' own websites, and a clear way to [compare your options](/#directory). You can look at every product HMRC recognises and filter the list if you are a landlord, self-employed, using a spreadsheet, in the Construction Industry Scheme, or looking for a free option. I use AI to gather and organise the details quickly, but a person always checks the results before anything goes on this site.

Where I earn a commission, or could, I'll always tell you. Those products are shown in a labelled Featured section, and the facts about every product are checked the same way.
