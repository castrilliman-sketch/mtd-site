BRIDGING SOFTWARE, EXPLAINED
What is MTD bridging software?

Bridging software connects the spreadsheet where you already keep your records to HMRC's Making Tax Digital system, so you can file quarterly updates without switching to full accounting software. aligned.tax is free, HMRC-recognised bridging software for filing your MTD for Income Tax quarterly updates: save or export your records as an XLSX workbook, upload it, review the figures, and submit.

With aligned.tax, filing your own MTD quarterly updates is free, always - no card, no time limit. See how it works with Excel, or start from a free landlord bookkeeping template orself-employed bookkeeping spreadsheet.

FAQ
Common questions
Is aligned.tax HMRC-recognised software?

Yes. aligned.tax is officially HMRC-recognised software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, recognised in July 2026. That means it passed HMRC's production approval checks and files through HMRC's official MTD API. 'Recognised' is the term HMRC uses - HMRC does not approve or endorse individual products. Read the announcement.

Is there free MTD software?

Yes. aligned.tax is free, HMRC-recognised MTD software for Income Tax - filing your own quarterly updates is free, always, with no credit card. Accountants and bookkeepers get their first three clients free, then simple per-client pricing. A few accounting packages offer free tiers too, and GOV.UK lists every compatible option. What "free" really means under MTD.

How much does MTD software cost?

Bridging software typically costs £5 to £10 +VAT per month - £72 to £144 a year - and full accounting packages run from around £10 to £40+ per month. With aligned.tax, filing your own MTD quarterly updates is £0, always, with no card. See the pricing page for the full picture.

Will HMRC provide free software for Making Tax Digital?

No. HMRC does not provide its own filing software for MTD for Income Tax - you must use compatible third-party software, which GOV.UK lists. Free options exist, including aligned.tax, which is HMRC-recognised and free for individuals filing their own quarterly updates.

What is MTD ITSA?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment. From April 2026, sole traders and landlords earning over £50,000 must keep digital records and submit quarterly updates to HMRC. The threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027.

Do I have to change my spreadsheet?

No. That's the whole point. Upload whatever you've got, even if it's messy. Smart Map reads your spreadsheet and figures out what goes where. You review the mapping, make any tweaks, and submit.

Can I use Excel or a spreadsheet for MTD?

Yes. HMRC lets you keep your records in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet, as long as you file through software that connects to HMRC. aligned.tax does exactly that: you upload your spreadsheet, review the figures, and we submit your quarterly update. Here is how it works with Excel.

How long can I use bridging software?

As long as you need to. Using a spreadsheet with bridging software is a valid, ongoing way to meet Making Tax Digital, not a temporary workaround, and there is no time limit on it. More on spreadsheets and MTD.

How does Smart Map work? Is my data sent to AI?

Smart Map sends a prepared view of your spreadsheet to an AI model so it can suggest how cells map to HMRC fields. The view includes spreadsheet structure, labels and financial figures, but recognised personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and National Insurance numbers are redacted before the request is sent. Your original spreadsheet file is never sent. The model returns cell references only, and you review every suggestion before anything is submitted to HMRC. Every request runs under a zero data retention policy with data collection denied, enforced on the request itself, so it can only be answered by an endpoint that does not retain your data, and inputs are not used to train models.

What happens to my spreadsheet after I submit?

Your choice. You can store it securely on our platform for your records, or have it deleted immediately after submission. Either way, we keep only the summary data that was sent to HMRC. Nothing more.

You can also delete your entire account and all associated data at any time from your account settings. No hoops, no retention period, no need to contact support.

What about year-end submissions?

Year-end filing support is in active development and will be available soon. You won't need a separate platform. Quarterly and annual submissions will all be handled in aligned.tax.

What's the HMRC soft landing period?

MTD for Income Tax is a legal requirement from April 2026. You still need to keep digital records and submit quarterly updates. However, HMRC's soft landing means no penalty points for late quarterly submissions in year one (April 2026 to April 2027). It's time to get set up, not time to ignore it. aligned.tax is free for individuals filing their own updates.

Is this just for accountants?

Not at all. aligned.tax works for anyone who needs to file under MTD ITSA: sole traders, landlords, and accountants managing clients. The multi-client dashboard is particularly useful for practices, but if you're filing for yourself it's just as simple. Our help centre has step-by-step guidance for each account type.

Will aligned.tax stay free?

For individuals, yes. Filing your own MTD Income Tax quarterly updates is free, always - a commitment, not a launch offer. For accountants and bookkeepers, everything is free until April 2027 and the first three clients stay free forever; the per-client pricing that applies from the 2027/28 tax year is published in full on the pricing page.

Is free MTD software safe? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Filing your own quarterly updates is free, and always will be - no credit card required. If you are an accountant, practice pricing is published openly: first three clients free, then a simple per-client price. The core filing route stays free; optional extras, such as one-to-one setup help, are priced separately. Your connection to HMRC is secure, you review every figure before anything is submitted, and your data is encrypted with two-factor authentication on every account.

I use Google Sheets, not Excel. Can I still use aligned.tax?

Yes. In Google Sheets, go to File > Download > Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) and upload the downloaded file. The same applies to Apple Numbers (File > Export To > Excel) or LibreOffice Calc (Save As > .xlsx). aligned.tax needs an .xlsx file, but any spreadsheet app can export one in a few clicks.

How is my data protected?

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We enforce two-factor authentication on all accounts and maintain a complete audit trail. You choose whether aligned.tax keeps your original spreadsheet after submission or deletes it. For Smart Map, only the redacted prepared view described above leaves aligned.tax, with zero data retention enforced on every AI request.

Missed a quarterly update? It's not too late to catch up.

There are no penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026-27. Updates are cumulative, so your next update also covers any quarter you missed.
