What is ‘Making Tax Digital’?
HM Revenue & Customs wants to cut its costs, reduce errors, get paid quicker and bring its systems and those of taxpayer businesses into the digital age. Meaning you’ll need recognised making tax digital bridging software like ours.

HM Revenue & Customs believes the software industry is better suited to assist businesses bring their affairs into the digital age, so quarterly filing will need to be submitted to HMRC via recognised third-party HMRC bridging software such as 123 Sheets. From April 2026 HM Revenue & Customs will require sole traders and landlord businesses to keep their bookkeeping on MTD accounting software (eg: Microsoft Excel) and make quarterly submission of income and expense summary totals via HMRC-recognised third-party MTD ITSA software, where their gross income was over £50,000 in the prior tax year.

Those of turnover between £30,000 to £50,000 start from April 2027. Those of turnover between £20,000 and £30,000 start from April 2028. HM Revenue and Customs has not yet announced dates for partnerships. VAT-registered persons and entities must already keep digital bookkeeping and make regular digital submissions of data to HMRC using third-party VAT Return software. Limited companies are not currently slated to join MTD for corporation tax any time soon.

What is ‘Making Tax Digital’?

There are two basic elements to this:

1. You must keep your bookkeeping, through to the digital filings, on software (eg a spreadsheet). You cannot do it in ledger books/paper anymore;
2. If your bookkeeping software doesn’t have a facility for you to file digital sumbissions to HMRC, you will need bridging software. This is what we provide.

What Data do you Send to HMRC?

We extract out of your spreadsheet the summary figures to be sent to HMRC. HMRC do not get your spreadsheet. We do not keep anything you upload to us.

Are Spreadsheets only a Temporary Solution for MTD?

No, our software has been recognised by HM Revenue & Customs for long-term use to accept spreadsheets.

Don’t get sucked in by the tales of some software providers that spreadsheets are only a temporary solution.

How Do I Insert The 123 Sheets Worksheet Into My Spreadsheet?

‍Watch this video For Excel

‍or follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have both spreadsheets open in Excel: our worksheet and your bookkeeping spreadsheet;
2. Then, click on "View" then "View Side-by-Side". You may need to click on "Reset Window Position". You should now see both spreadsheets at the same time on the same screen: one above the other;
3. Now pick up by the tab and drop either our worksheet into your spreadsheet, or vice versa to finish.

Failing that, you do not need to merge our worksheet into yours. You could instead keep the two spreadsheets separate, and use a formula to move your summary HMRC figures to our spreadsheet and just upload that.

Is Your Software Compatible With Older Versions of Microsoft Excel?

Our software is compatible with Microsoft Excel 1997 through to the latest versions. However, if you have chosen to use our bookkeeping spreadsheet, you'll need Excel 2016 or higher.

Don’t I need to Put My Business Details in The Spreadsheet?

No. In order to file a Return, you will set up an account with us and enter those details there. When filing a Return to HMRC, we send those details and your figures to HMRC, so we do not need to ask you for them each time you submit a Return.

Do you Work with Sage, Quickbooks and Other Bookkeeping Software?

Yes, just export your summary data to Excel, and we can submit your data to HMRC. We have a specific VAT solution for Sage and Quickbooks users on our VAT page. As long as your software allows you to export the Return into Excel or a .csv file, you can use our software. If it will only export a PDF, you can use a free PDF converter on the internet to convert your file into an Excel file. We are looking to increase the number of automatic imports for different software. If your software isn’t listed, get in touch, and we may be able to provide an automatic import facility for you within a week or so.

Do you Work with Different VAT Schemes?

Yes, we work with all VAT Schemes.

FRS, Groups, Partial Exemption, Capital Goods, Margin Schemes, Cash Accounting, Annual Scheme, Retail Schemes, Payments on Account etc can all use our software. Just ensure the figures on your return are calculated correctly according to your scheme rules and are put in the correct boxes as per the rules for your VAT scheme. (Eg: on the FRS, Box 6 is the Gross Sales (inc. VAT), and Box 1 is your FRS % multiplied by your box 6 figure – other boxes are normally zero).
