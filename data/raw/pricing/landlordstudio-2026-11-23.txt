FAQs
What is Landlord Studio?

Landlord Studio is an all-in-one property management and accounting software built specifically to help landlords and small portfolio investors get ready for Making Tax Digital (MTD). Our award-winning platform simplifies rental accounting, automates income and expense tracking, and generates digital records and HMRC-ready reports, ensuring you're fully prepared for MTD requirements.

With Landlord Studio, landlords can easily:

Stay MTD-ready by securely storing digital records, automating quarterly submissions, and ensuring accurate tax reporting.
Automate rental accounting to reduce manual entry, errors, and time spent managing finances.
Effortlessly track income and expenses, providing real-time financial insights to help you maximise profits and reduce your tax bill.
Manage your portfolio from anywhere with our user-friendly mobile app and cloud-based software.

Thousands of UK landlords rely on Landlord Studio to simplify their transition to Making Tax Digital, save time, and optimise their rental business.

Who is Landlord Studio for?

Landlord Studio is designed specifically for independent landlords, small portfolio investors, and property managers who want an easy way to comply with HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) rules without complicated or expensive software.

Whether you own a single rental property or manage a portfolio of multiple properties, Landlord Studio simplifies digital record-keeping, quarterly submissions, and HMRC compliance, helping you easily transition away from spreadsheets and manual processes.

If you're impacted by MTD requirements, tired of manual data entry, or looking for an affordable way to meet your tax obligations, Landlord Studio provides the tools you need to stay organised, compliant, and financially efficient.

What’s the difference between the Go and Pro plans?

Our Go plan provides landlords managing up to 3 properties with all the essential tools they need to replace their spreadsheets and streamline their operations.

Our Pro plan, on the other hand, unlocks powerful automation, reporting, and management tools that save you time, help maximise rental income, and stay compliant.   

With Pro, you get:
‍
✅ Automated bank feeds for effortless expense tracking
✅ Advanced reporting to optimise cash flow and tax deductions
✅ Lease tracking with reminders for renewals and rent increases

Upgrading means less manual work and more confidence in managing your rentals.

Can I try the Pro plan before committing?

Yes! Everyone gets a 14-day FREE trial of the Pro plan when you sign up (no credit card required). Explore all the premium features before making a decision. If it’s not the right fit, you can always switch back to Go without losing your data.

How does pricing work?

Our pricing is simple and transparent. You pay a flat monthly or annual fee based on the number of units you manage. No hidden fees, and you can cancel anytime.

What happens if I go over my property limit?

If you add more properties than your plan allows, we’ll notify you and give you the option to upgrade and add your new unit to your subscription. You’ll always have access to your data, and we make upgrading seamless.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! There are no long-term commitments. Cancel or downgrade your plan at any time, and you’ll still have access to your data.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes! Save up to 20% when you choose annual billing instead of monthly payments.

Is my data safe with Landlord Studio?

Absolutely. We use bank-grade encryption to protect your financial data and comply with the latest security standards. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

Do you offer support if I need help?

Yes! Our customer support team is here to help via email, chat, and our knowledge base. Pro users also get priority support for even faster responses.

For those who want premium support from a dedicated Account Manager our Pro PLUS plan is for you!

Do you work with Accountants?

Yes, Landlord Studio partners closely with accountants to support landlords in meeting their Making Tax Digital (MTD) obligations. Our Accountant Partner Program is designed to help accounting firms streamline client onboarding, simplify quarterly submissions, and ensure accurate digital record-keeping for landlord clients.

If you're an accountant looking to support your landlord clients through MTD with simple, compliant software, learn more about our Accountant Partner Program here.
