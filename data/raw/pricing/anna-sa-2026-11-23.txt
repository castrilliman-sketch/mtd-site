Making Tax Digital – explained

Making Tax Digital for Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) is now live. If you're a sole trader or landlord earning over £50,000, quarterly reporting to HMRC is now required. Here's everything you need to know – and how ANNA does it for you, free.

What is MTD for Income Tax (MTD for ITSA)?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is HMRC’s digital way of reporting what you earn. Instead of one big Self Assessment return once a year, you keep your records digitally, send HMRC 4 quarterly updates, and finish the year with a final declaration.

You’ll see it called all sorts of things – MTD for Income Tax, MTD ITSA or MTD for Self Assessment. They all mean the same thing. And with ANNA, all of it happens behind the scenes.

Does MTD apply to you? The 3 thresholds

It depends on your qualifying income, and it’s being phased in over 3 years.

MTD for Income Tax start dates by qualifying income
WHEN IT STARTS	QUALIFYING INCOME	STATUS
6 April 2026	Over £50,000	Live now
6 April 2027	Over £30,000	Next
6 April 2028	Over £20,000	Announced

Under £20,000? You stay on Self Assessment as things stand.

HMRC looks at your tax return from 2 years earlier to determine when you need to join MTD. For 2026/27 (6 April 2026), this means your 2024/25 return.

If you are required to join, you should sign up for MTD as you are responsible for checking and then to register. HMRC may also contact you or sign you up automatically.

What actually changes: 4 updates and a final declaration

You used to do one return, once a year, usually in a January panic. Now you keep digital records all year, send HMRC a summary of your income and expenses every 3 months, then tie it all together with a final declaration.

The quarterly updates are just summaries. They’re not tax returns, and you don’t pay anything on them.

4 updates instead of 1 return sounds like more admin, and it is. But it also means no January guesswork – you’ll know what you owe as you go. Here’s the rest of the upside: 7 benefits of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

Your MTD deadlines for 2026/27
MTD for Income Tax quarterly update deadlines for the 2026/27 tax year
QUARTER	PERIOD	UPDATE DUE
1	6 April – 5 July 2026	7 August 2026
2	6 July – 5 October 2026	7 November 2026
3	6 October 2026 – 5 January 2027	7 February 2027
4	6 January – 5 April 2027	7 May 2027
Final declaration	2026/27 tax year	31 January 2028

Prefer round months? You can ask HMRC to use calendar quarters instead, so your periods end on the last day of the month.
