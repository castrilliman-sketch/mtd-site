What is MTD ITSA?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) is HMRC’s system requiring Sole Traders and Landlords with income above a threshold to submit quarterly updates digitally, followed by the usual annual Self Assessment. Learn more about MTD ITSA.

When do I need to start using Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA)?

If you’re a Sole Trader earning over £50,000, you’ll need to comply by April 2026. Sole Traders earning over £30,000 will need to comply by April 2027.

What do I need to do for Making Tax Digital?

First, check if MTD applies to you—right now it covers all VAT-registered businesses, and Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) is next. Then you’ll need MTD-compatible software to record your income and expenses and submit your returns.
