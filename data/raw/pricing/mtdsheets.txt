Your Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common questions, so you can file with confidence.

What is Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?
Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is HMRC’s new digital reporting system for sole traders and landlords. Instead of only reporting income once a year, affected taxpayers must keep digital records, send quarterly updates to HMRC, and complete their tax return using compatible software.
Who needs to use MTD for Income Tax?
MTD for Income Tax applies to sole traders and landlords whose qualifying income is above the HMRC threshold. Qualifying income is broadly your total income from self-employment and property before expenses.
When does MTD for Income Tax start?
MTD for Income Tax starts in phases. It applies from 6 April 2026 if your qualifying income is over £50,000, from 6 April 2027 if it is over £30,000, and from 6 April 2028 if it is over £20,000.
Can I use Excel for MTD Income Tax?
Yes. You can use Excel or another spreadsheet for MTD Income Tax if your records are kept digitally and connected to HMRC using compatible software. MTD Sheets is designed for users who want to keep using spreadsheets and submit Income Tax updates to HMRC.
What is MTD Income Tax bridging software?
MTD Income Tax bridging software connects your spreadsheet records to HMRC. It allows you to keep your income and expense figures in Excel, then submit the required quarterly updates through Making Tax Digital compatible software.
Do I need expensive bookkeeping software for MTD Income Tax?
No. You do not necessarily need expensive bookkeeping software for MTD Income Tax. If you already keep good spreadsheet records, bridging software such as MTD Sheets can be a simpler and lower-cost way to submit your updates to HMRC.
What are quarterly updates for MTD Income Tax?
Quarterly updates are summaries of your self-employment or property income and expenses. They are sent to HMRC every three months using compatible software. They are not the same as a full tax return.
When are MTD Income Tax quarterly updates due?
For standard update periods, quarterly updates are usually due by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. Your software should show the correct obligations and deadlines for your HMRC record.
Do landlords need MTD Income Tax software?
Yes, landlords may need MTD Income Tax software if their property income, combined with any self-employment income, is above the relevant HMRC threshold. MTD Sheets helps landlords keep using spreadsheets and submit property income updates digitally.
Do sole traders need MTD Income Tax software?
Yes, sole traders, freelancers and self-employed individuals may need MTD Income Tax software if their qualifying income is above the relevant threshold. MTD Sheets is suitable for sole traders who already track income and expenses in a spreadsheet.
Can accountants use MTD Sheets for Income Tax clients?
Yes. Accountants can use MTD Sheets to support clients who keep records in spreadsheets. It is useful for firms that want to help sole traders and landlords comply with MTD Income Tax without forcing every client onto complex bookkeeping software.
Is MTD Sheets HMRC-recognised?
Yes. MTD Sheets is HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital software. It is built for spreadsheet users who want a simple way to upload, review and submit MTD Income Tax updates to HMRC.
