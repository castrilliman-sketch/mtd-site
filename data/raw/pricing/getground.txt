Making Tax Digital is the UK Government's plan to modernise the tax system, requiring landlords and self-employed individuals to keep digital records and send quarterly updates to HMRC instead of a single annual tax return.

Transactions must be digitally recorded using accounting or bookkeeping software.

Quarterly updates will be compulsory to keep tax estimates accurate

You are still responsible for completing and submitting your year-end tax return

Paying any tax due must be completed by 31st January following the end of the tax year.
