How in-year MTD ITSA updates work

In-year updates give HMRC information about business or property income and expenses during the tax year. They are not the same as the final tax return, so you should continue to complete any required end-of-year and final submission.

Capture income

Important: Eligibility, reporting frequency and deadlines depend on your circumstances and the current HMRC rules. Please check the latest HMRC guidance or speak to a qualified tax adviser if you are unsure.

Frequently asked questions
Is iFiler HMRC recognised?

Yes. iFiler is listed on HMRC’s official Making Tax Digital software finder, which means HMRC recognises it as compatible software for MTD submissions. HMRC does not approve or endorse any individual product, so recognised means listed as compatible, not recommended by HMRC.

Can I file MTD for Income Tax without bookkeeping software?

Yes. iFiler is bridging software, so you can keep your records in a spreadsheet and use iFiler to send your quarterly updates to HMRC. You do not need to buy or learn full bookkeeping software to meet the Making Tax Digital for Income Tax requirements.

How does spreadsheet bridging work?

You prepare your income and expense figures in your spreadsheet, enter them into iFiler, review the totals, and iFiler submits them digitally to HMRC through its Making Tax Digital connection. Your figures reach HMRC without being retyped into a form, and your spreadsheet stays your record.

Is iFiler free?

Yes, iFiler is currently free to use for VAT returns and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax quarterly updates. Some upcoming modules will be charged when they launch, and this answer will be updated at that time.

What can I file with iFiler?

You can file VAT returns and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax quarterly updates. Annual returns and the Books module with open banking are in testing and are not yet available, so this page will be updated when they launch.
