Frequently asked questions
Is TaxPig secure?

Absolutely. We use high level encryption, direct secure connections to HMRC over HTTPS and regular vulnerability & penetration tests

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. TaxPig is fully responsive and works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer.

Do I still need an accountant?

TaxPig lets you handle your tax returns yourself. But if you prefer you can opt to use our accountants to check and submit your returns, or invite your own accountant to manage your TaxPig account.
