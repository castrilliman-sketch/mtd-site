Your Making Tax Digital Ready Kit

Every MTD for Income Tax deadline from now to January 2029, what to do before you start, and a simple routine for each quarter. Every date comes from GOV.UK.

1. When do you start?

It depends on your qualifying income: your self-employment plus property income before expenses, from the tax return for the earlier year.

6 April 2026Over £50,000 in 2024 to 2025
Already live
6 April 2027Over £30,000 in 2025 to 2026
Based on the return due 31 January 2027
6 April 2028Over £20,000 in 2026 to 2027
Based on the return due 31 January 2028

£20,000 or less: automatically exempt. You still send a Self Assessment return as normal. You also still send a normal Self Assessment return for the tax year before you start MTD.

5. Getting-ready checklist

Do these before your first quarterly update. Most people can do them in an afternoon once the software is chosen.

  • Work out your qualifying income. Add your self-employment and property income before expenses from your latest tax return. Salary, dividends, pensions and partnership profit don't count.
  • Find your start date using the table on page 1. HMRC writes to people it expects to qualify, but if you don't get a letter it is still your responsibility to check.
  • Check whether you're exempt. £20,000 or less is automatically exempt. Some people, for example those who are digitally excluded, can apply for an exemption.
  • Choose HMRC-recognised software that covers your income types (self-employment, UK or foreign property), and check it can submit your tax return too, or pair it with one that can.
  • Decide: standard or calendar update periods. Use calendar periods (1 April to 31 March) only if that is your accounting period. Set it in your software before your first update, because you can't change it for the rest of the tax year.
  • Sign up for MTD for Income Tax on GOV.UK with your Self Assessment sign-in details, or ask your agent to. Have your business name, address, trade and any recent start dates ready.
  • Authorise your software to talk to HMRC and connect your bank feeds or spreadsheet.
  • Start digital records from your first day in MTD: every income and expense item, by category.
  • Jointly let property? Decide whether to include expenses in each quarterly update or add them at year end by resending update 4.

6. Every quarter

  • Bring records up to date for the period (all income and expenses, by category).
  • Check the totals your software works out. Each update covers the tax year so far, so fix earlier mistakes in your records and they flow into the next update.
  • Send the update for each business (each self-employment and your property business), even if nothing happened in the period.
  • Check your HMRC online account says "You sent a quarterly update".
  • Look at the tax estimate in your software and put money aside.

7. After the tax year ends

  • After update 4 (due 7 May), make any accounting and tax adjustments, and resend update 4 if your records changed.
  • Add your other income and gains (for example savings interest, dividends, partnership profit). HMRC adds some items, such as PAYE and pensions, but you must check them.
  • Submit your tax return through your software by 31 January.
  • Pay your tax by 31 January (and 31 July if you make payments on account). MTD does not change payment dates.
