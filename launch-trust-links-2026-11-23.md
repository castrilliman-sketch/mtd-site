# Launch trust links: UK directories and communities (researched 23 Nov 2026, nothing submitted)

For mtdsoftwarecompare.co.uk (operator Daniel Wyatt, contact customerservices@mtdsoftwarecompare.co.uk).
Researched from each directory's own pages where they loaded, otherwise from recent independent write-ups (named). Nothing has been submitted or signed up for. Re-check each page before signing up; terms change.

**Key fact for this site:** it is an online-only business with no premises and no customer visits. Map-style listings (Google Business Profile, Bing Places) are built for businesses that meet customers in person, so they are not the right fit. Most general directories ask for a UK address and phone; **do not put a home address on public listings**. Decide first whether D has a business address (registered office / mail address) and a business phone, then use the exact same name, website and email on every listing.

Suggested one-line description (same everywhere): "MTD Software Compare is a free, independent UK comparison of HMRC-recognised Making Tax Digital for Income Tax software for sole traders and landlords, run by Daniel Wyatt."
